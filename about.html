Rooted in Bengal, made by hand.

Why we started

In 2019 we watched a market vendor hand over a plastic bag that would outlast her grandchildren. Jute, the golden fibre of Bengal, has carried rice and grain for centuries. We brought it back as a bag people want to carry.

Today we work with 60 weavers across Howrah and Murshidabad. Each bag is stitched from start to finish by a single artisan.
